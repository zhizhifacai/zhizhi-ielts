🧁素材A · 表姐的烘焙工作室
从家庭厨房到小店 · 手工与创业  人物·创业手工技能攒钱与服务
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
My cousin Lily runs a small bakery studio. She started by baking cakes at home for friends, just as a hobby, and later saved up for almost two years to open a tiny shop near a subway station. She is really good with her hands — she can pipe cream, fold dough and design a cake from a rough sketch. The shop only has three small tables, but she makes everything herself early in the morning before opening. What I admire most is that she never rushed. When the rent went up, she didn't quit; she started selling online to keep the business alive. She is very organised too, with a notebook for orders, costs and regular customers. Last year I saved money to buy her a proper cake mixer, and she still uses it every day. She also treats customers well — she remembers regulars' names and always adds a small free cookie.
📖 中文对照
我表姐开了一家很小的烘焙工作室。她一开始只是在家给朋友做蛋糕，纯当爱好，后来攒了将近两年钱，才在地铁站附近开了家小店。她手特别巧，会裱花、揉面，还能照着草图设计蛋糕。店里只有三张小桌子，但每天开店前她都会一个人把所有东西做好。我最佩服的是她从不急躁。房租涨的时候她没放弃，而是开始做线上销售，把生意撑了下来。她也很有条理，用一本笔记本记录订单、成本和老客户。去年我攒钱给她买了一台像样的打蛋机，她到现在每天都用。她对客人也好，记得熟客的名字，还总会送一块免费小饼干。

🏞️素材B · 成都三日之旅
城市·自然·拥挤 · 一次旅行的多面  地点旅行自然与城市
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
Two years ago I took a three-day trip to Chengdu. It is a big, lively city, and I would happily go back. In the daytime we visited an old teahouse in a park by the river, where local people played cards and drank tea under the trees — it felt calm and green even inside the city. At night we went to a famous hotpot street, and honestly it was the most crowded place I have ever been to. We waited over an hour for a table, and it was so noisy that we had to shout to hear each other. On the last day we took a short bus ride to a mountain just outside the city; the air was fresh and there were bamboo forests everywhere. We spent very little money — the bus was cheap and entrance was free. I would recommend Chengdu to anyone, mainly for the food and the relaxed pace. But the trip also had one dull part: the long highway back, which was a boring, endless stretch of grey road.
📖 中文对照
两年前我去成都玩了三天。那是一座热闹的大城市，我很愿意再去。白天我们去河边公园里一间老茶馆，当地人在树下打牌喝茶，即使在城里也觉得很安静、很绿。晚上我们去了有名的火锅一条街，说实话那是我去过最挤的地方。等位等了一个多小时，还特别吵，说话都得喊。最后一天我们坐了趟短途巴士到城外的山，空气很新鲜，到处都是竹林。我们没花什么钱——巴士很便宜，门票也免费。我会把成都推荐给任何人，主要是为了吃的和那种悠闲的节奏。这趟旅行也有无聊的一段：返程的高速公路，一望无际的灰色路面，特别乏味。

📷素材C · 摄影迷莉莉
爱拍照的快乐人 · 本地小名人  人物·热情兴趣名人
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
Lily is a friend of mine who is crazy about photography. She always carries a camera, even to buy groceries, and she takes photos of ordinary things — old doors, street cats, steam from a noodle stall. She is honestly one of the happiest people I know. She is never bored, because to her everything is worth a shot. Last year her photos of the old town were shared by a local news account, and now people in our area recognise her as "the girl who takes photos". She is a bit of a local celebrity, though she is very humble about it. I met her only once at a small photo walk, and I immediately wanted to know more about her, because she explained the whole city to me through her lens. She once interviewed a famous street photographer and wrote a post about it. I really admire how she turns a normal afternoon into something worth keeping.

🗣️素材D · 帮同学练口语
帮助他人 · 语言学习 · 小组合作  学习帮助改变
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
My classmate Wang was really weak at speaking English. He could read and write, but the moment he had to speak, he froze. So I offered to practise with him every evening for a month before an important exam. We worked in a small group of three, and that made him less nervous. I didn't teach grammar; instead I made him speak about simple things first — his day, his family, his favourite food — until the words came out without thinking. It was actually a challenge for me too, because I had to explain things in easy English. Slowly he changed his opinion: he used to believe he was "just bad at languages", but after six weeks he could hold a five-minute conversation. That experience changed my own opinion as well — I had always thought talent mattered most, but I learned that practice and a safe partner matter more. Later I used the same method when I taught my little cousin to introduce herself in English.
📖 中文对照
我同学小王口语特别弱。他读写没问题，但一要开口就卡住。于是重要考试前一个月，我每天傍晚陪他练。我们是三个人小组练，这样他就没那么紧张。我没讲语法，而是先让他说简单的事——他的一天、家人、喜欢的食物——直到不用想就能说出来。这对我也算是挑战，因为我得用简单的英语解释。慢慢地他改变了看法：他以前觉得自己「就是没语言天分」，但六周后他就能做五分钟的对话了。这件事也改变了我自己的看法——我以前以为天分最重要，后来发现练习和一个让人安心的搭档更重要。后来我把同样的方法用在我小表妹身上，教她用英语自我介绍。
✨ 亮点表达
freeze 卡住 practise with him 陪他练 a safe partner 让人安心的搭档 hold a conversation 完成一段对话 change one's opinion 改变看法 practice matters more 练习更重要

🏃素材E · 晨跑与作息改造
健康习惯 · 早起 · 改变与计划  健康早起改变
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
My brother is the healthiest person I know. He gets up at half past five every morning and runs for forty minutes before work, rain or shine. He also goes to bed before eleven and cooks his own meals instead of ordering takeaway. I used to think he was just lucky, but then I tried to copy him for a month. I had to change my plan a lot — I used to stay up late scrolling on my phone, and that was a real time-waster. I set my alarm earlier, laid out my running shoes the night before, and started small, just fifteen minutes. The first two weeks were painful, but soon getting up early felt normal. It also changed my daily routine: I started eating breakfast properly and finishing work earlier. Now I see that a healthy routine isn't about being strict; it's about small habits you keep.
📖 中文对照
我哥是我认识的最健康的人。他每天早上五点半起床，上班前跑四十分钟，风雨无阻。他也十一点前睡觉，自己做饭而不是点外卖。我以前觉得他只是运气好，后来我试着照做了一个月。我不得不经常改计划——我以前爱熬夜刷手机，那真是个浪费时间的事。我把闹钟调早，前一晚把跑鞋摆好，从小做起，只跑十五分钟。头两周很痛苦，但很快早起就变得很正常了。它还改变了我的日常作息：我开始好好吃早饭，工作也提前完成。现在我觉得健康作息不是严苛，而是靠坚持的小习惯。

🏛️素材F · 老城图书馆与河畔
建筑 · 河湖 · 城市里的自然  地点·建筑自然文化
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
My favourite building in my city is the old library by the river. It was built over a hundred years ago, with tall windows, a stone staircase and a quiet reading hall that smells of old paper. I go there whenever I want to think. The building faces a wide river, and on sunny days the light on the water comes through the windows and moves across the floor. I also love to visit my grandparents' home in the countryside — it is warm and full of memories, but I wouldn't want to live there, because it is far from everything and the internet is terrible. As for tall buildings, there is one glass tower downtown that I dislike: it looks cold and blocks the view of the old library from the bridge. The river that the library sits on is actually the most important one in my city — it used to carry goods and now it is where people walk and jog at weekends.
📖 中文对照

🎬素材G · 一部电影与一本书

It is not complicated, but it made me happy because the writing is warm and funny, and the dog reminded me of my own childhood pet. There is also a short video I found online about an old man who repairs clocks; it is only five minutes long but it stuck with me for weeks. And there is one advertisement I remember well: a famous basketball player appears in it, but instead of selling shoes, he quietly gives them to kids on a playground. That ad impressed me because it told a story instead of shouting "buy this".

♻️素材H · 家乡垃圾分类新规
环保法 · 当地新闻 · 长期目标  社会法律目标
🎤 范文（英文可按段背）
In my hometown, the local government introduced a new rule about rubbish sorting: every area now has four coloured bins, and people who mix their rubbish can be fined. It made the local news, and old people at the market talked about it for weeks. At first many people complained, because it was inconvenient. But slowly it worked — the streets are cleaner and the recycling rate has gone up. I would introduce one more law: a rule that all new buildings must have solar panels and a small green roof. It is not a huge change, but over ten years it would cut a lot of energy use. My long-term ambition is actually related to this: I want to work in environmental policy, so I have set myself a goal of studying hard, learning about law, and maybe working for a green organisation one day. It is a big decision, but it is one I feel good about. I once faced a challenging technical problem when our recycling app crashed, and I had to fix it under pressure.
📖 中文对照
在我家乡，当地政府推出了垃圾分类新规：每个小区现在都有四个不同颜色的垃圾桶，混投会被罚款。这事上了本地新闻，菜市场的老人聊了好几个星期。一开始很多人抱怨，因为不方便。但慢慢地有效了——街道更干净，回收率也上去了。我还想再推动一条法律：所有新建筑都必须装太阳能板和小型绿色屋顶。这不是什么巨变，但十年下来能省很多能源。我的长期目标其实和这个有关：我想从事环境政策工作，所以给自己定了目标——好好学习、研究法律，将来也许去一家环保机构。这是个重要决定，但我觉得是对的。有一次我们的回收App崩溃，我面对一个很棘手的技术问题，还得在压力下把它修好。
✨ 亮点表达

💡 使用方法

1. 第 1 周：每天背 1 段素材（英文为主，中文对照理解），重点记"人物 + 一件具体的事"。
2. 第 2 周：对着每段素材下的"能套的题"，口头切换着说一遍，练"改开头、改重点"。
3. 考前：只背 ✨ 亮点表达 + 每题的"怎么套"一句话，把 8 段故事当骨架。
4. 素材里的具体细节（名字、地点、数字）可根据自己真实经历替换，越 personal 越自然。
